About Us

Our Mission

Slowly Reverb is an open-source, client-side audio processing tool designed for music enthusiasts, producers, and experimental creators.

Our goal is to provide a free, accessible, and private way to apply "slowed + reverb" effects to audio files without requiring complex software, accounts, or uploads to external servers.

How it works: Unlike traditional web apps, Slowly Reverb uses the native Web Audio API built into your browser. When you upload a file, it never leaves your device. The audio is decoded, processed (time-stretched and convolved with a synthetic reverb impulse response), and encoded back into an MP3 file entirely within your browser's memory.

Open Source Commitment: We believe in transparency. This project is open-source, meaning the code is publicly available for review, contribution, and self-hosting. We do not track, store, or monetize your audio data.
