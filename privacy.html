Privacy Policy

Your Data Stays With You

Last Updated: October 2026

1. No Data Collection

Slowly Reverb is designed with a "privacy-first" architecture. We do not collect, store, transmit, or process any audio files, metadata, or personal information on our servers.

2. Local Processing

All audio processing occurs entirely within your web browser using the Web Audio API. Your uploaded files never leave your device. Once you close the browser tab, all temporary processing data is permanently cleared from your device's memory.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics trackers, or third-party monitoring tools. We do not track your usage patterns, IP address, or browsing behavior.

4. Third-Party Libraries

This application uses the open-source lamejs library (loaded via CDN) for client-side MP3 encoding. This library operates entirely within your browser and does not communicate with any external servers.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please reach out through our official GitHub repository.
